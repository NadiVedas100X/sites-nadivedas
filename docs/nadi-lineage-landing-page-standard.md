# NadiVedas lineage landing-page standard

Use this brief for every dedicated Nadi lineage page.

- Match or exceed the depth, visual storytelling and conversion quality of the Shiva Nadi page.
- Treat each page as a premium high-consideration sales experience, not a basic information page.
- Use at least three relevant, optimized WebP visual moments: hero, lineage/story and leaf-search/process.
- Keep body text comfortably readable on mobile and desktop; establish consistent hierarchy and avoid dense, undersized copy.
- Explain the lineage story, the search process, what makes the Nadi distinct, reading coverage, pricing, refund condition, reviews and FAQs.
- Label oral lineage accounts and religious tradition as traditional belief rather than verified historical or scientific fact.
- Use lineage-specific fees throughout the page, journey and server-side payment calculation.
- Never show the generic ₹999 header or footer CTA on a lineage-specific page. Use a lineage-specific mobile sticky CTA.
- Make the first payment the clear primary action. Explain the higher reading fee only after value, process and trust have been established.
- Optimize visual crops, spacing, typography, touch targets and sticky actions specifically for mobile.
